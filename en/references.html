Editorial note

Didactic adaptation, not a verbatim reproductionThe concepts, caveats, examples, and figures are preserved, but the material has been reorganized into learning tracks, summarized, and explained with original diagrams. Consult the source PDF for the complete wording.

The terminology is still young. In this guide, agent harness means the runtime and control machinery immediately surrounding a model. Harness engineering is the broader discipline of designing interfaces, context, state, execution, controls, feedback, recovery, and verification.

Quick glossary

AgentA model operating in an action loop toward a task.
HarnessRuntime and control machinery driving that loop.
SessionA logical history and state container; durability depends on implementation.
ContextInformation supplied to a particular model invocation.
ToolA callable capability exposed to the model.
SandboxA controlled execution environment.
WorkflowAn execution structure largely specified by code.
Evaluation harnessInfrastructure that runs and grades evaluations.
